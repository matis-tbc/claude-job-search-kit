# Standard Answers

One place for every answer an application form asks again and again. Claude fills forms from this table only. Change a row here and every future form changes with it. Numbers trace to the [[Fact Sheet]]. Anything marked **[YOU]** is decided by you each time. Essays come from the [[Story Bank]], not from here.
